# ADR-039: Unani Clinical Knowledge Mapping & Organ-Axis Relationship Engine (Prompt 20)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** October 2026
- **Context:**
  Prompt 20 establishes a source-grounded, carefully governed mapping layer for Unani concepts, clinical observations, organ/system concepts, and the product's Organ-Axis framework. A pervasive hazard in clinical knowledge graphs is "relationship inflation"—treating navigational groupings, classical poetic functional associations, or semantic proximity as proof of modern pathophysiological causality or therapeutic efficacy. Furthermore, automated graph traversal often erroneously infers transitive relationships ($A \to B \to C \implies A \to C$). A robust clinical architecture must distinguish documented classical concepts, clinician-entered observations, modern cross-disciplinary findings, and product navigation models, strictly prohibiting autonomous diagnostic recommendations or unprovenanced causality.

- **Decision:**
  1. **Controlled Relation-Type Registry (`relation_type_registry`):** All knowledge predicates are registered in a governed registry. Each predicate specifies relation class (`source_documentation`, `terminology`, `identity_crosswalk`, `unani_knowledge`, `modern_clinical`, `product_navigation`, `patient_specific`), subject and object concept type constraints, directionality, clinical actionability flag, mandatory source assertion flag, required reviewer roles (`UNANI_SCHOLAR`, `CLINICAL_SPECIALIST`), and allowed intended uses.
  2. **Provenance-Bound Relationship Assertions (`relationship_assertions`):** Relationships between concepts are explicitly typed assertions, not mere graph adjacency. Substantive clinical relationships (`classical_organ_association`, `indicated_for_mizaj_condition`, `contraindicated_in`) require link to an authentic source assertion (`assertion_id` -> `knowledge_assertions`). Each relationship is tagged with an epistemic level (`ui_navigation_grouping`, `classical_text`, `clinician_authored_case_hypothesis`, `reviewed_evidence_based`, `research_hypothesis`) and review state (`draft`, `approved_for_defined_use`, `disputed`, `withdrawn`).
  3. **Versioned Organ-Axis Frameworks (`organ_axis_frameworks`):** The Organ-Axis model is formalized as an explicit, versioned product navigation model (`product_navigation_model`). It carries mandatory metadata detailing its purpose and clinical limitations. Product navigation frameworks are barred from receiving `approved_use_scope = 'clinical_decision_support'`.
  4. **Organ-Axis Concept Memberships (`organ_axis_memberships`):** Links concepts to functional axes (e.g. `GASTRO_HEPATIC_AXIS`, `BRAIN_GUT_AXIS`). Distinguishes `navigation_group` from `source_documented_membership` and `reviewed_clinical_membership`, requiring assertion links for clinical claims.
  5. **Cross-Disciplinary Concept Mappings (`concept_mappings`):** Crosswalks to modern biomedical terminologies (ICD-11, SNOMED-CT, POWO) strictly preserve mapping semantics (`exact`, `broader_than`, `narrower_than`, `related_to`, `candidate_equivalence`, `no_safe_mapping`). Entries marked `candidate_equivalence` are quarantined from autonomous clinical decision support.
  6. **Relationship Conflicts Adjudication (`relationship_conflicts`):** Conflicting assertions between sources or authorities enter a formal adjudication queue (`source_disagreement`, `interpretation_disagreement`, `scope_mismatch`). Resolutions require recorded rationale and authorized clinical scholar review.
  7. **Encounter-Scoped Case Hypotheses (`case_hypothesis_links`):** Clinician hypotheses recorded during patient consultations remain protected patient health records tied to `encounters(id)`. They are strictly isolated and never written back to the global ontology graph.
  8. **Bounded Graph Traversal & Non-Inference Rule:** Graph traversal is strictly bounded (max depth 3) with cycle detection. UI navigation edges and unreviewed assertions are filtered out in clinical mode. Transitive clinical inference is strictly prohibited: intermediate paths are returned with provenance and an explicit caveat stating that reachability does not prove transitive causality or efficacy ($A \to C$).
  9. **Backward Compatibility:** Preserves existing Prompt 04 `/api/v1/organ-axis/concepts` and `/api/v1/organ-axis/mappings` endpoints without breaking existing clinical consultation workflows.

- **Consequences:**
  - Complete elimination of hallucinated or assumed clinical relationships.
  - Transparent epistemic distinction between UI navigation groupings and source-grounded clinical knowledge.
  - Strict protection of patient encounter hypotheses from leaking into public or global ontology models.
  - Total adherence to Prompt 20 non-negotiable rules.
