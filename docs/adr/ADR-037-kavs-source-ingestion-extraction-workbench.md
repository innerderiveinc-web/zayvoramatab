# ADR-037: KAVS Phase K2–K3 — Verified Source Ingestion, OCR Fidelity & Claim Extraction Workbench

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** October 2026
- **Context:**
  Prompt 18 defines the implementation requirements for Phase K2 and K3 of the Knowledge Authenticity, Verification & Clinical Safety Framework (KAVS). The framework strictly forbids automated invention or hallucination of formulations, posologies, historical units, botanical taxonomy, contraindications, or page references. Digital transformation of classical Unani texts, official pharmacopoeias (UPI, NFUM), and modern clinical guidelines requires rigorous source rights preflight verification, cryptographic file integrity checking (SHA-256 + magic bytes), a server-authoritative 21-state ingestion lifecycle, explicit separation between physical PDF page indices and printed page numbering, automated high-risk clinical pattern detection, a human-supervised transcription correction workbench, and candidate claim drafting mathematically quarantined from live consultation retrieval until dual-human clinical promotion.

- **Decision:**
  1. **Source Rights Preflight Engine (`kavs_rights_preflight_events`):** Mandates four discrete operational permissions (`RETAIN_FILE`, `EXTRACT_TEXT`, `INDEX_VECTORS`, `EXPORT_REDISTRIBUTE`) before any source file can be stored, parsed, or embedded. Violations immediately halt processing.
  2. **Cryptographic Source File Vault (`kavs_source_files`):** Restricts uploads to verified MIME types (`application/pdf`, `image/tiff`, `image/png`, `application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.wordprocessingml.document`). Enforces magic-byte validation (`%PDF-`, `PK\x03\x04`, `\x89PNG\r\n\x1a\n`) and SHA-256 checksums to detect corruption or tampering.
  3. **Server-Authoritative 21-State Ingestion Lifecycle (`kavs_ingestion_jobs`, `kavs_ingestion_job_events`):** Strict, non-skippable transition pipeline from `queued` -> `rights_preflight` -> `file_stored` -> `integrity_check` -> `text_layer_detected` -> `ocr_running` -> `layout_analyzed` -> `reading_order_normalized` -> `table_extracted` -> `footnotes_linked` -> `segmentation_running` -> `high_risk_flagged` -> `transcription_review` -> `transcription_approved` -> `claim_drafting` -> `candidate_claims_created` -> `clinical_review_queued` -> `completed` (with `stalled`, `cancelled`, and `failed` failure paths).
  4. **Physical vs. Printed Pagination Disambiguation (`kavs_extraction_pages`):** Distinguishes `pdf_page_index` (zero/one-based physical file offset) from `printed_page_label` (the folio number stamped on the page), preventing offset drift caused by front matter, introductions, and indexes.
  5. **Automated High-Risk Clinical Scanner:** Regex and heuristic inspection for:
     - Numerical dosages and dose ranges (`NUMERIC_QUANTITY_OR_RANGE`)
     - Historical Unani posology units such as *masha*, *tola*, *dirham*, *misqal*, *ratti*, *dam*, *habba* (`HISTORICAL_POSOLOGY_UNIT`)
     - Botanical Latin binomial taxonomy with author citations (`BOTANICAL_TAXONOMY`)
     - Safety contraindications and therapeutic prohibitions (`SAFETY_CONTRAINDICATION_OR_NEGATION`)
     - Classical Arabic and Urdu scripts (`ARABIC_URDU_SCRIPT`)
     - Low OCR confidence threshold (< 0.80) triggering `REQUIRES_MANUAL_TRANSCRIPTION`.
  6. **Human Transcription Correction Audit Trail (`kavs_extraction_corrections`, `kavs_extraction_segments`):** Machine extractions remain `machine_raw` until corrected or verified by a qualified human reviewer, transitioning to `human_checked` with a mandatory rationale. Unreadable passages are permanently marked `unreadable` and barred from claim generation.
  7. **Candidate Claim Drafts Isolation Gate (`kavs_claim_drafts`):** Drafts generated by machine extraction or OCR parsers live in `kavs_claim_drafts` and are mathematically excluded from `knowledge_claims` and consultation retrieval. Promotion to the atomic clinical claims registry requires that the underlying extraction segment achieves `human_checked` fidelity; upon promotion, claims enter the atomic registry in `UNDER_REVIEW` state requiring KAVS Phase K1 dual-scholar sign-off before clinician exposure.
  8. **Zero Legacy Dependencies:** All capabilities operate exclusively inside the new standalone repository.

- **Consequences:**
  - Complete provenance chain from physical ink on paper to atomic clinical claim in the consultation orchestrator.
  - Mathematical prevention of unreviewed AI extractions polluting the clinical safety firewall or prescription engine.
  - Zero tolerance for OCR dosage hallucination or unit drift in classical Unani medicine formulations.
