# ADR-017: Investigation Considerations, Referral Records, and Cryptographic Sign-Off

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Context:**
  Clinical decision support often blurs the line between educational investigation considerations and legally binding laboratory/imaging orders. Generating unauthorized lab requisitions or premature outbound referral dispatches without practitioner verification violates clinical protocol. Additionally, completed modern assessments and referrals must be immutable legal medical records protected against retroactive tampering.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Strict Non-Ordering Contract for Investigation Considerations:** Investigation suggestions generated by rule evaluation carry an explicit non-ordering contract (`is_order: false`, `requires_clinician_order: true`). Suggestions can only be accepted, modified, or rejected by the clinician and never automatically create billable or lab-dispatched diagnostic orders.
  2. **Governed Emergency and Routine Referral Documentation:** Outbound referrals (`referral_records`) capture urgency tiers (`EMERGENT`, `URGENT`, `ROUTINE`), clinical reason, facility/specialty destination, patient handover status, and transport instructions, with tenant isolation and Patient 360 timeline indexing.
  3. **Cryptographic PIN Sign-Off and Canonical Digest:** Finalising a modern clinical assessment requires:
     - Clinician digital signature PIN verification (`verify_pin` against practitioner profile).
     - Deterministic canonical JSON serialization of history, vitals, problems, acknowledged red flags, differential decisions, and referrals.
     - SHA-256 fingerprint generation recorded as `signature_digest`.
     - Status transition to `SIGNED` locking records from in-place mutation.
  4. **Formal Amendment Lifecycle:** Corrections to signed assessments require creating a versioned amendment record (`clinical_assessment_amendments`) capturing prior state, rationale, updated snapshot, and signing practitioner ID.
- **Consequences:**
  - Provides ironclad non-repudiation, tamper-evident auditability, and regulatory compliance for medical documentation.
  - Ensures clean operational demarcation between clinical guidance and actual diagnostic ordering.
