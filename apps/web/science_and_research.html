Pharmacological Standardization, Detoxification (Tadbeer) Kinetics, and Safety Ceiling of Suranjan (Colchicum autumnale L.) in Modern Arthropathies

Prof. (Dr.) Mansoor Alam, Dr. Irfan Ashraf, ZAYVORA Safety Taskforce

Suranjan Talkh (crude Colchicum corm) contains 0.45%–0.55% active colchicine, carrying narrow therapeutic index risks. This paper establishes the kinetic detoxification protocol (Tadbeer in milk and vinegar) yielding standardized Majun Suranjan with 0.08 mg bio-active colchicine per gram. We formalize the non-negotiable 30-day cumulative ceiling of 12.0 mg active alkaloid exposure to prevent hepatorenal toxicity while retaining potent tubulin-dimer inhibition in inflammatory gout and crystal arthropathies.
